# Terms

G&G provides scientific reference and analysis. It does not sell cannabis, arrange delivery, or give cultivation, feeding, pest or harvest instructions.

Estimates are not probabilities unless the server marks a calibrated probability. A chemical median is not a guarantee. A generated image, when one exists, is a visualization, not a photograph of offspring.

Digital subscriptions, if they are offered later, will be sold through Google Play Billing and verified on the server. No subscription is on sale in the current build.
